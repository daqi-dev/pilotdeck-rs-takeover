# spike

Write the hypothesis, acceptance observation and stopping point first. Use an isolated workspace and representative input, including a likely failure. Record commands, environment and observed output. Distinguish a demonstrated capability from a simulated dependency. Finish with a supported verdict and the remaining uncertainties. Do not promote a prototype to production or modify shared deployment state without a separate implementation task.
